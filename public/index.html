Our Story

From humble beginnings
to global reach

EduMitra started with a simple belief — that every student deserves a clear, confident path forward. What began as a small counselling initiative has grown into one of India's most trusted names in education and career guidance.

Today, we've helped over 5,000 students across 10+ countries find their direction — from stream selection at 16 to career pivots at 40. We don't just give advice; we walk the journey with you.

Our team of expert counsellors brings deep global exposure, psychometric expertise, and genuine care to every single session. Because your future deserves nothing less.
